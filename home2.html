Operational Precision

Our Daily Routine Timeline

Here is how we orchestrate seamless facility cleanliness every single day.

06:00 AM — Dawn Inspection Shift

Supervisors sweep standard touch points, verify air ventilation, and open the cleaning checklist logbooks before corporate arrivals.

09:00 AM — Day Porter Checklists

Cleaning staff clean common corridors, sanitize elevator keys and railings, and manage cafeteria trash receptacles.

02:00 PM — Midday Sanitization Shift

Targeted sanitization sweeps of washrooms, conference tables, glass surfaces, and reception lobbies.

07:00 PM — After-Hours Deep Clean

Vacuum extraction of corporate carpets, high-pressure sanitizing of ceramic surfaces, window restoration, and waste clearing.
