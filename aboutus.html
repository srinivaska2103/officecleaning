Our Heritage

Our Journey to Workspace Excellence

Founded in 2011 by industry veterans, CleanPro started with a simple vision: to bring scientific precision, corporate integrity, and strict accountability to commercial janitorial operations. We recognized that businesses needed more than just a surface clean—they required a sterile, healthy environment where their teams could excel without compromise.

Over the years, we have scaled our services from small corporate spaces to large multistory skyscrapers, medical laboratories, and logistics hubs. We invest heavily in our workforce, green seal chemical solutions, and structural auditing technologies. Every janitor is fully vetted and receives ongoing training on CDC and OSHA sanitization standards.

Today, we stand as a trusted facility management partner, employing over 250 cleaning professionals and servicing hundreds of active spaces daily. Our operations run round-the-clock, allowing us to fit into your specific business calendar without causing disruptions.
