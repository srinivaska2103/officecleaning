Quality Assurance

Our 5-Step Photo QA Verification Process

Every cleaning shift is photo-documented and reviewed by a dedicated QA supervisor before the client is notified. No space is marked complete without photographic sign-off.

1

Pre-Clean Inspection Photos

Supervisor photographs the space before work begins to document initial condition and set cleaning scope.

2

Active Cleaning Documentation

Team leaders capture photos of each major task completed: floors, restrooms, kitchens, and common areas.

3

Post-Clean QA Walkthrough

QA supervisor conducts a final walkthrough and photographs each zone against our standardized cleaning checklist.

4

Digital Report Upload

All photos are uploaded to the client's secure portal dashboard with timestamps and crew member IDs.

5

Client Approval & Sign-Off

Client receives a completion notification and reviews photos before digitally approving the shift as complete.
